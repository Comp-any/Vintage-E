== What other mods did at start-up ==
• Other mods: 1 recipe(s) or drop(s) name items that don't exist (by mod of the missing item: fromgoldencombs (1)).
  The game ignores those recipes, so Vintage-E can't learn from them. Usually the mod named expects another mod, or an older version of one, to be installed.
