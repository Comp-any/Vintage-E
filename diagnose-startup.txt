== What other mods did at start-up ==
• Other mods: 5 were skipped by the game because something they need isn't installed: autoconfiglib (needs configlib (Dependency 'configlib_1.13.2.zip' (configlib) has dependency errors itself)), configlib (needs vsimgui@1.2.0), playermodellib (needs overhaullib@2.0.10), sandwich (needs expandedfoods, aculinaryartillery), vintageskavenrat (needs playermodellib@* (Dependency 'playermodellib_1.25.0.zip' (playermodellib) has dependency errors itself))
  The game leaves these mods out entirely, so this doesn't break Vintage-E, but their items and recipes don't exist in this world and can't have values.
  → Install what they need from the Mod DB (the name after 'needs' is a mod id), or remove them.
• Other mods: 9 recipe(s) or drop(s) name items that don't exist (by mod of the missing item: armory (9)).
  The game ignores those recipes, so Vintage-E can't learn from them. Usually the mod named expects another mod, or an older version of one, to be installed.
