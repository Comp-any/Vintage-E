== A machine's power cost isn't in the value, or looks wrong ==
A value only includes a recipe's power when that recipe is the cheapest way of making it, and the power's price comes from your setting, the mod's engines or the mod's suggestion. Hold the item.
Hold the block or item, then run /emc diagnose hand (or click the question below).
✓ Power prices: 2 energy unit(s) in use, each with a price (2 listed)
  An energy unit's price comes from your EnergyEmc setting first, then from what the mod's own engines burn to make it (a base value ÷ the energy made), then from the mod's suggested price. With none of these its power costs nothing.
  vintagee:testgen (generator power): 2 EMC a unit, from the test engine: 1 charcoal (32 EMC, base value) make 16 → 2 EMC each
  vintagee:testpower (test power): 2 EMC a unit, from vintagee's suggested price
  Heat costs 0.0006154 EMC per °C·s (the median of 5 reference fuels' value per °C·s (charcoal, coke, ore-lignite, …)).
/emc dump hand shows every cost line of the recipe behind a value with where its price came from.
