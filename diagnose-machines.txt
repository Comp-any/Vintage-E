== Chutes and hoppers ==
✓ Alchemical Chest: chutes and hoppers fill it from any side; a chute underneath empties it.
  As with the game's chests. A hopper never takes from a block above it (it only catches items dropped on top): use a chute to take things out.
✓ Energy Condenser: chutes and hoppers fill its storage (never the target slot). Only finished copies of the target come out: through a chute or hopper underneath, or a straight chute on its side that leads into a chest.
  With no target set, what goes in is burned into the condenser's EMC. Look at a condenser and ask again to see what each chute touching it does.
✓ Matter furnaces: from above into the input (things that smelt or cook), from the sides into the fuel, a chute underneath takes the output.
  This needs a change to the game's furnace inside; it's in place on this server.
• Transmutation Table: a chute or hopper into it puts in things to burn into its owner's EMC (16 a second, owner online only); nothing comes out.
  Klein stars never go in by chute (a charged star would be burned); collectors and relays take a star only by hand.
