== Recipes that make EMC out of nothing ==
If a recipe's output is worth more than its ingredients, a player can craft it and burn it for free EMC, over and over. The mapper prevents this for values it works out itself; trader prices, guesses, fallback values and pins can break that guarantee.
⚠ 3 items can be made for less than they're worth (3 of them by processing):
  Temporal gear (game:gear-temporal): worth 4,096, costs 624.8: 3,471.19 free each time (processing: smelting, grinding, drying...)
    Temporal forge (Vintage Engineering) (vinteng): 1 vinteng:metalgear-steel (+0.75 energy) -> 1 game:gear-temporal
    Trace its value down to the raw materials (/emc diagnose trace game:gear-temporal)
  Charcoal (game:charcoal): worth 64, costs 32: 32 free each time (processing: smelting, grinding, drying...)
    Creosote oven (Vintage Engineering) (vinteng): 1 game:log-placed-acacia-ud -> 2 game:charcoal (and 11 more recipes with the same output)
    Trace its value down to the raw materials (/emc diagnose trace game:charcoal)
  Wood-Tar Creosote (vinteng:creosoteportion-wood): worth 48, costs 32: 16 free each time (processing: smelting, grinding, drying...)
    Creosote oven (Vintage Engineering) (vinteng): 1 game:log-placed-oak-ud -> 25 vinteng:creosoteportion-wood + 1 game:charcoal (and 1 more recipes with the same output)
    Trace its value down to the raw materials (/emc diagnose trace vinteng:creosoteportion-wood)
  → Raise the ingredient that's too cheap (usually the one whose value is a guess or a trader price) with /emc set, or pin the output at what its ingredients cost. The trace shows the whole chain.
