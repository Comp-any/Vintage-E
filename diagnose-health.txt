• Players: nobody is online to check.
  A player's EMC and learned items are stored with the player, so only online players can be checked. Run this while players are on.

✓ Solver: every recipe whose ingredients all have values gave its output one.
✓ Trader prices: no item is priced from a trader above what a recipe for it costs.
⚠ Variants: 42 value(s) were copied from a look-alike variant in a family that doesn't agree (color (40), rock (1), model (1)).
  When nothing makes an item, the mapper copies the value of a variant that differs only in look (a copyAcross group). That's safe when the family agrees. Here the family's own-valued members disagree by more than half, so the copy took whichever member came first, and the item may be worth a lot more or less than it says. An attribute behind many of these (growth stage, age, state) isn't look-only at all and belongs in neverAcross.
