  A player's EMC and learned items are stored with the player, so only online players can be checked. Run this while players are on.
• Value swings: no earlier mapping to compare with yet.
⚠ Solver: 1 item(s) have a recipe whose ingredients all have values, yet got no value.
  The mapper saw the recipe and every ingredient is valued, so only the solver itself stopped the output: the item sits in a value loop it couldn't settle, or its value came out below the smallest the solver keeps (a recipe that gives back far more than it takes, like a block uncrafted into shingles, drives it there).
  Copper plate (game:metalplate-copper) ← grid: 4x game:slantedroofing-copper-east-free (in a loop)
  → Pin the item at what its ingredients add up to (the trace shows them), and include the list in a bug report: this is exactly the kind of case the solver should handle.
✓ Trader prices: no item is priced from a trader above what a recipe for it costs.
⚠ Variants: 130 value(s) were copied from a look-alike variant in a family that doesn't agree (color (61), cover (48), colour (20), rock (1)).
  When nothing makes an item, the mapper copies the value of a variant that differs only in look (a copyAcross group). That's safe when the family agrees. Here the family's own-valued members disagree by more than half, so the copy took whichever member came first, and the item may be worth a lot more or less than it says. An attribute behind many of these (growth stage, age, state) isn't look-only at all and belongs in neverAcross.
