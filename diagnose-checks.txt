== Recipes that make EMC out of nothing ==
If a recipe's output is worth more than its ingredients, a player can craft it and burn it for free EMC, over and over. The mapper prevents this for values it works out itself; trader prices, guesses, fallback values and pins can break that guarantee.
⚠ 1 items can be made for less than they're worth (0 of them by processing):
  Bone (game:bone): worth 24, costs 8: 16 free each time
    grid: 1 butchering:strongbone -> 1 game:bone
    Trace its value down to the raw materials (/emc diagnose trace game:bone)
  → Raise the ingredient that's too cheap (usually the one whose value is a guess or a trader price) with /emc set, or pin the output at what its ingredients cost. The trace shows the whole chain.
  Every recipe that gives more EMC than it costs (/emc diagnose profit)
