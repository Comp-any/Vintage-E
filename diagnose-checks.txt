== Metal tiers out of order ==
⚠ 1 families are worth more in a cheaper metal than in a dearer one:
  The order is this server's own: the metals ranked by what their ingot is worth (copper, the bronzes, iron, meteoric iron, steel, with the gold-bearing black bronze wherever its ingot lands). A family where the cheaper metal's version is worth more usually lost an ingredient in one recipe, or took a guessed value in the other.
  Tin bronze block (riveted) (game:metalblock-new-riveted-tinbronze) 1,689.6 > Chromium block (riveted) (game:metalblock-new-riveted-chromium) 512
    grid: 1 game:metalplate-tinbronze + 1 game:metalplate-tinbronze + 1 game:metalplate-tinbronze + 1 game:metalplate-tinbronze + 1 game:metalplate-tinbronze + 1 game:metalplate-tinbronze -> 1 game:metalblock-new-riveted-tinbronze
  → Open the dearer one's trace: the odd ingredient is in there. A wrong value for a metal itself is fixed in the settings (BaseValues).
