== Recipes that make EMC out of nothing ==
If a recipe's output is worth more than its ingredients, a player can craft it and burn it for free EMC, over and over. The mapper prevents this for values it works out itself; trader prices, guesses, fallback values and pins can break that guarantee.
✓ No recipe gives more EMC than its ingredients cost.
