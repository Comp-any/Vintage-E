== Floral Zones: Caribbean Region (floralzonescaribbeanregion) ==
198 of its 211 blocks and items have a value (93.8%); 0 are blacklisted on purpose (creative, technical or story items).
Vintage-E has no compatibility file for 'floralzonescaribbeanregion'. Its raw materials only get a value if a recipe makes them, you pin them, or the mod gives them an emcValue attribute.
