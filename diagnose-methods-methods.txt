== Other mods' crafting methods ==
Other mods can tell Vintage-E about their machines and processes: what each recipe uses and makes (with chances), the tools it wears, and what else it costs (power, heat, machine time). Each recipe below shows what it uses and makes, what one costs at today's values, and whether it gives something its value, is beaten by a cheaper way, or waits for a value it needs.
✓ Crafting methods: 1 mod(s) send them (0 by file, 1 by code): 2 methods, 6 recipes
  A mod sends its crafting methods in a file (assets/<modid>/config/vintagee-methods/) or from its code (the game's event bus, no reference to Vintage-E needed). docs/crafting-methods.md in Vintage-E's repository describes the format.
  Vintage-E (vintagee): code, format 1: 2 methods, 6 recipes (0 left out, 0 warnings)  look (/emc diagnose methods vintagee) check (/emc diagnose methodcheck vintagee)
✓ Power prices: 2 energy unit(s), each with a price
  An energy unit's price comes from your EnergyEmc setting first, then from what the mod's own engines burn to make it (a base value ÷ the energy made), then from the mod's suggested price. With none of these its power costs nothing.
  vintagee:testgen (generator power): 2 EMC a unit, from the test engine: 1 charcoal (32 EMC, base value) make 16 → 2 EMC each
    the test engine: 1 charcoal (32 EMC, base value) make 16 → 2 EMC each
  vintagee:testpower (test power): 2 EMC a unit, from vintagee's suggested price
  Heat costs 0.0006 EMC per °C·s (the median of 5 reference fuels' value per °C·s (charcoal, coke, ore-lignite)).
⚠ Free power: 1 recipe(s) in use make more than 1 EMC a second from power alone (up to 20)
  Windmills and water wheels make power for nothing. A recipe whose value includes its power cost turns that power into EMC when what it makes is burned, so a high price makes a machine an EMC farm. The limit is a quarter of an Energy Collector MK1 (4 EMC a second).
  vintagee:selftest-press/stone: 200 EMC of power in 10 s = 20 EMC a second
  Lower vintagee:testpower to 0.1 EMC a unit (the most that stays under the limit) (in the window)
  Switch vintagee:selftest-press off (in the window)
