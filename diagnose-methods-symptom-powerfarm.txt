== Players make EMC out of free power ==
Windmills make power for nothing; a high power price turns it into EMC when what the machine makes is burned.
⚠ Free power: 1 recipe(s) in use make more than 1 EMC a second from power alone (up to 20)
  Windmills and water wheels make power for nothing. A recipe whose value includes its power cost turns that power into EMC when what it makes is burned, so a high price makes a machine an EMC farm. The limit is 1 EMC a second, a quarter of what an Energy Collector MK1 makes (4 EMC a second).
  vintagee:selftest-press/stone: 200 EMC of power in 10 s = 20 EMC a second
  Lower vintagee:testpower to 0.1 EMC a unit (the most that stays under the limit) (in the window)
  Switch vintagee:selftest-press off (in the window)
  → Lower the unit's price, switch the method off, or turn ChargeMethodEnergy off.
  Set ChargeMethodEnergy to false (in the window)
A lower price, or no power cost at all, closes it; values made with power then go down.
